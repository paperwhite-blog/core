You cloned PaperWhite and ran `paperwhite dev`. Everything you see is generated from the Markdown in `content/`, styled by the built-in **paper** theme.

> [!tip] Three things to do next
> 1. Run `paperwhite init` to set your title, URL and deploy target, and to remove these sample posts.
> 2. Write with `paperwhite new "My first post"`, or point `contentDir` in `paperwhite.config.yaml` at your Obsidian vault.
> 3. Push to GitHub. The workflow `init` wrote builds and deploys the site.

Notes link to each other with wikilinks: [[Writing in Obsidian]] shows every syntax that renders, [[Customizing your blog]] covers themes and plugins, and [[Publishing]] explains deploying and scheduled posts. The [[About]] page is a page rather than a post, so it has no date and does not appear in feeds.

Backlinks, related posts, the table of contents, search, RSS, sitemaps and social cards are all generated for you. Open the page source if you are curious: there is almost no JavaScript in it.
